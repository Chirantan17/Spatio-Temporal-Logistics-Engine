1. Customer Drop-Off Gate Validation

Verifies that the selected hostel/academic gate sits inside an authorized campus polygon before accepting checkout.

Brute-Force: 4.8 ms Indexed: 0.14 ms

Why Brute-Force Spikes

Without a spatial index, PostgreSQL runs polygon point-in-poly math across every boundary row sequentially. GiST prunes non-overlapping bounding boxes immediately.

Key Indexing & Query Short Notes

GiST (2D Spatial): R-Tree bounding boxes on location_trace & campus_zone; accelerates KNN <-> & ST_Contains.
BRIN (Time-Series): Block Range Index on append-only recorded_at; 97.8% smaller than B-Tree (24 KB vs 1,112 KB).
Partial Index (Hot-Path): Indexes only active orders & AVAILABLE drivers in 8.0 KB RAM; ignores historical deliveries.
Query Optimizations: DISTINCT ON for single-pass latest GPS ping, <-> before spherical math, and SKIP LOCKED for deadlock-free dispatch.
